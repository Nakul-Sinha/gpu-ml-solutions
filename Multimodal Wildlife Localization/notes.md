# Multimodal Wildlife Localization: Notes

## Task
- Predict normalized bbox around single animal (cow/deer/horse) in paired RGB + thermal 300x300 crops.
- Metric: 100 * (0.65*(1-IoU) + 0.35*coord_MAE). LOWER is better.
- 173 train / 67 test. Tiny dataset -> pretrained backbone + heavy aug + CV essential.

## Key findings
- Thermal is a COLORMAP (not grayscale). Animal reads brighter in thermal than grass/soil in 91% of
  train crops (RGB shows animal darker than background in 83%). Strong complementary cue.
- Naive thermal thresholding gives only ~0.18 IoU -> a learned multimodal model is required.
- Box centers ~0.5, mean area 0.17 (animals fill a good fraction of the crop).
- 6-channel early fusion (RGB+thermal) into ImageNet ResNet34, conv1 inflated to 6 channels.
- Aerial top-down => flips + 90-degree rotations are label-preserving augmentations (with box transform).
- Crop-around-box augmentation and heavy photometric jitter HURT (v2 CV ~30.5 vs v1 ~27.7).

## Result
- 5-fold CV (ensemble + flip/rot TTA): metric = 28.45 +- 1.35 (IoU ~0.6).
- Test box-area distribution matches train (mean 0.18 vs 0.17) -> predictions well calibrated.
- Valid 67-row submission (finite, in [0,1], x_min<x_max, y_min<y_max).

## Compliance
- Uses only provided train boxes + paired image arrays. No AWIR source metadata, filenames, or private labels.
- Learned CNN (not rule-based thresholding). Deterministic. torch/torchvision/sklearn only.

## Files
- solution.py: self-contained, reads ./dataset[/public]/, writes ./working/submission.csv (5-fold ensemble + TTA).
- submission.csv: 67 rows, validated. Generated by running solution.py on the H100.
