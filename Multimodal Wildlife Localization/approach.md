I framed this as direct bounding box regression from the paired sensors. I stacked the red green blue crop together with the thermal crop into a six channel input, because the animal appears warmer and brighter than grass and soil in the thermal view, which gives a strong localization cue that colour alone misses. I used an ImageNet pretrained residual network with its first layer widened to accept six channels, followed by a small head that outputs the four box edges. I trained with a loss that combines generalized intersection over union with an absolute error term so it matches how the challenge scores boxes. Because the crops are aerial and top down I augmented with flips and ninety degree rotations with matching box updates. I averaged five models and added flip based test time augmentation.
