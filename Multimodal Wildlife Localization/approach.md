I framed this as direct bounding box regression from the paired sensors. I stacked the red green blue crop together with the thermal crop into a six channel input, because the animal appears warmer and brighter than grass and soil in the thermal view, a cue that colour alone misses. I used an image net pretrained residual network with its first layer widened to accept six channels, followed by a small head that outputs the four box edges. I trained with a loss combining generalized intersection over union with an absolute error term so it matches how boxes are scored. Because the crops are aerial and top down I augmented with flips and rotations. I then averaged five models with flip based test time augmentation.
