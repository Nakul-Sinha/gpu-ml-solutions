# Shadow-Based GPS + Time Prediction — Notes

## Task
- Predict latitude, longitude, UTC hour from a single 512x512 shadow image; doy provided.
- Metric: 0.5*geoscore + 0.5*timescore, geoscore=1/(1+mean_haversine_km/500), timescore=1/(1+mean_hourerr/3).
- 5600 train / 2400 test. Baselines: random ~0.18, all-zeros(lat0,lon0,hr12) ~0.23.
- Rules: NO analytical sun-position solving, NO shadow-angle measurement, NO ephemeris. Learn implicitly.

## Approach (compliant, learned)
- ImageNet ResNet50 backbone + doy sin/cos conditioning -> regress lat (direct) and lon/hour (circular sin/cos).
- Stable sin/cos MSE loss under mixed precision. Mild photometric aug only (flips/rotation would corrupt the
  sun-azimuth cue, so they are NOT used).
- Per-target calibration: blend each prediction with the training prior at a weight chosen on a held-out split
  to maximize the exact competition score (also controls the >10,000 km anti-cheat penalty on longitude).

## Investigation & key finding (why the ceiling is low)
Thorough experiments (5+ backbones/resolutions incl. ResNet34/50 at 224/288/336, ConvNeXt, strong+light aug,
per-target probes) show the CNN MEMORIZES training labels but generalizes essentially ZERO on held-out data:
- val latitude MAE stays ~= the mean-predictor baseline (~31 deg) across every config.
- val hour MAE and longitude MAE never beat their constant-prediction baselines.
This is consistent with two structural facts:
1. **Physical coupling**: the image reveals latitude and LOCAL solar time (LST). But UTC hour and longitude are
   coupled as LST = hour + lon/15, so they cannot be separated from one image. An ORACLE that knows exact
   (latitude, LST) still scores only ~0.28 (measured on train). Exact-hour + best-lon caps at ~0.55.
2. **Injected noise**: the generator deliberately adds noise to sun placement / shadow angle / atmosphere to
   prevent extraction, which (combined with 5600 images) leaves too little signal for a CNN to learn the
   implicit inverse mapping.
The described "physics-aware ~0.70" appears to require information beyond shadow geometry (a generation leak,
which the rules explicitly say to report and NOT exploit). So the compliant ceiling here is ~0.24-0.28.

## Result
- Held-out competition score: prior 0.2348 -> calibrated model 0.2362 (blend lat=0.95, lon=0.75, hour=prior).
- Valid 2400-row submission; lat/lon are image-conditioned, hour uses the calibrated optimal constant
  (UTC hour is unrecoverable without longitude, so its mean is the Bayes-optimal prediction).

## Compliance
- Pure image-conditioned CNN. No sun-position equations, no shadow-angle measurement, no ephemeris, no leak
  exploitation. Trains only on the provided training set. Deterministic. torch/torchvision/numpy/pandas only.
