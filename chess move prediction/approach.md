I treated this as learning a calibrated outcome distribution from the opening moves. I wrote a small self contained parser that replays the moves to rebuild the board, then extracted features that generalize across cohorts such as material balance, captures, castling, checks and piece development, since exact sequences never repeat in the test set. I also added opening family statistics by averaging historical results for games sharing the first few moves, computed out of fold to avoid leakage. I trained gradient boosted trees together with a multinomial logistic model, expanding each cohort into class observations weighted by how many games it represents. I blended the two models, shrank the probabilities gently toward the overall prior for stability, and fitted the confidence from the strongest class.
