I predict each cohort's outcome distribution by blending three complementary models. First I reconstruct the board from the moves with a small self contained parser and train a convolutional network on the piece placement together with the squares each side attacks, because king safety and piece activity decide these positions more than raw material does. Second I train a bidirectional recurrent network over the move tokens to capture the order and character of the opening. Third I estimate the historical result rate for each opening family using hierarchical shrinkage, alongside gradient boosted trees on material and development features. I average the three models, then shrink predictions toward the reliable opening rate wherever the models disagree or the opening is rare, so that unusual or uncertain lines cannot damage the worst group score. Finally I set the confidence from the strongest predicted class.
