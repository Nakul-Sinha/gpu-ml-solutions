I predict each cohort's outcome distribution by blending three complementary models. First I reconstruct the board from the moves with a small self contained parser and train a convolutional network on the piece placement planes, because the position reached after the opening carries strong signal that raw material counts miss. Second I train a recurrent network over the move tokens to capture the order and character of the opening. Third I estimate the historical result rate for each opening family using hierarchical shrinkage, combined with gradient boosted trees on material and development features. I average the three models, then shrink rare low support openings toward the overall prior so that unusual lines cannot damage the worst group score. Finally I derive the confidence from the strongest predicted class.
